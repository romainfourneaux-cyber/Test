                 Anglais - Corrigé des Exercices supplémentaires – Séquence 3


Ex. 1 : Écris en style indirect selon le modèle

I am studying (She / to say) → She says she is studying.
- We can’t see Big Ben (They / to say) They say they can’t see Bib Ben.
- I must write a letter (He / to think) He thinks he must write a letter.
- I want to be the queen. (She / to say) She says she wants to be the queen.
- We are laying the table. (They / to say) They say they are laying the table.
- I am always right ! (She / to think) She thinks she is always right.

Ex. 2 : Pose la question et réponds suivant le modèle
My brother / is tall and has got brown hair → What is your brother like ? He is tall and has got brown
hair.
- My new book / very interesting → How is your new book ? My new book is very interesting.
- His baby / lovely → How is his baby like ? His baby is lovely.
- Her husband / not young → How is her husband like ? Her husband is not young.

Ex. 3 : Réponds aux questions en utilisant un pronom interrogatif selon l’élément souligné. Sois bien
attentif au temps.
- It was five o’clock when he called me. What time was it when he called you ?
- I prefer the blue skirt. Which one do you prefer ?
- They went to New York last year. Were did they go last year ?
- It is sunny this afternoon. What’s the weather like this afternoon ?
- My best friend is 13 years old. How old is your best friend ?
- They stayed at home yesterday. Where did they stay yesterday ?
- They didn’t cut the grass because it was wet (mouillé) Why didn’t they cut the grass ?

Ex. 4 Mets les phrases suivantes au prétérit (progressif ou simple)
- They are having lunch together. They were having lunch together.
- They live in London. They lived in London.
- Do you want to lay the table ? Did you want to lay the table ?
- They have breakfast at the restaurant every Sunday. They had breakfast at the restaurant every
Sunday.

Ex. 6 : Complète les phrases suivantes avec des question-tags et des short answers. (sois bien
attentif au temps, à la personne, et à la ponctuation)
- We’ll need a new car, won’t we ? Yes, we will.
- My friend’s parents were going downstairs when they fell, weren’t they ? yes, they were.
- My father closed the door, didn’t they ? No, they didn’t.
- Sophie and I won’t try this skirt on, will they ? No, they won’t.
- I’m going to tell you a story, aren’t I ? Yes, you are.

Ex. 7 : mets les phrases suivantes aux formes interrogative puis négative (sois bien attentif au temps)
- They’ll go to the market.
Will they go to the market ? They won’t go to the market.
- The president is going to give a speech.
Is the president going to give a speech ? The president isn’t going to give a speech.
- He realizes what I mean.
Does he realize what I mean ? He doesn’t realize what I mean.
- They bought vegetables at the market.
Did they buy vegetables at the market ? They didn’t buy vegetables at the market.
