                 Anglais 5e - Exercices supplémentaires - Séquence 5

                                                Corrigés
Ex. 1 : Mets ces phrases au prétérit.
- He begins his homework, doesn’t he ? He began his homework, didn’t he ?
- I forget to call you, don’t I ? I forgot to call you, didn’t I ?
- Do you often speak with your neighbour ? Did you often speak with your neighbour ?
- He never takes sugar in his coffee. He never took sugar in his coffee.
- I don’t write when I am on holidays. I didn’t write when I was on holidays.

Ex. 2 : Rédige ces phrases au présent simple ou progressif ou au futur comme il convient, et complète
avec une question-tag.
- We will bring a cake at his birthday party tomorrow, won't we ?
- The children never go to school on Sundays, do they ?
- Look, There are a lot of people in this street, aren’t they ?
- Hey, can you help me, please : I’m trying to catch the bird. (pas de question-tag)

Ex. 3 : Relie les phrases suivantes selon le modèle, avec le pronom relatif who ou that.
You are the lady. You left your country. → You are the lady who left your country.
- She is the pianist. She played at the concert the day before yesterday.
She is the pianist who played at the concert the day before yesterday.
- The Darlings was the family. They lived in a lighthouse off an island in England.
The Darlings was the family who lived in a lighthouse off an island in England.
- Grace Darling was the girl. She was twenty-two years old in 1838.
Grace Darling was the girl who was twenty-two years old in 1838.
- Grace Darling was the girl. She saw an enormous ship on a rock.
Grace Darling was the girl who saw an enormous ship on a rock.
- Elizabeth II was the Queen. She officially opened the channel tunnel.
Elizabeth II was the Queen who officially opened the channel tunnel.
- It was Her Majesty's train. It entered the tunnel at Folkstone.
It was Her Majesty's train that entered the tunnel at Folkstone.
- The Channel tunnel is the tunnel. It as opened by the Queen in May 1994.
The Channel tunnel is the tunnel that was opened by the Queen in May 1994.

Ex. 4 : Pose la question liée à l’élément souligné, en utilisant un pronom interrogatif.

- I am thirteen years old. How old are you ?
- We’ll cross the Channel next month. When will you cross the Channel ?
- She is one meter ten. How tall is she ?
- We visited Wales because we wanted to see Cardiff. Why did she visit Wales ?
- Grace Darling saved nine people. How many people did Grace Darling save ?
- It costs 100 pounds to cross the Channel. How much does it cost to cross the Channel ?

Ex. 5 : Transcris les dates suivantes en lettres.
- 1666 : sixteen sixty-six
- 1994 : ninety ninety-four
- 2024 : two thousand and twenty-four
Ex. 6 : Mets ces phrases à la forme affirmative.
- Did she fall into the lake ? She fell into the lake.
- Did the Queen meet the French president ? The Queen met the French president.
- Did you have a lot of time to learn your lesson ? You had a lot of time to learn your lesson.
- Did he read this book ? He read this book.

Ex. 8 : Traduis les phrases suivantes.
- Il est plus dangereux que l’homme qui parle. He is more dangerous than the man who is talking.
- Je peux voir l’homme qui a arrêté le voleur. I can see the man who arrested the thief.
- Je me suis levé tôt. I got up early.
- Rencontreras-tu beaucoup de personnes ? Will you meet a lot of people ?
- Nous avons parlé avec l’homme qui a vu le feu d’artifice. We spoke / talked with the man who saw
