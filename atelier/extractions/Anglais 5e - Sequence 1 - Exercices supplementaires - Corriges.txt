                  Anglais - Corrigé des Exercices supplémentaires – Séquence 1


Ex. 1 : Mets ces phrases au présent simple ou progressif ou au prétérit progressif à la forme
interrogative puis négative (sans utiliser de pronom interrogatif)

- He plays in the garden. He doesn’t play in the garden. Does he play in the garden ?
- The Queen usually lives in Buckingham Palace. The Queen doesn’t live in Buckingham Palace. Does
the Queen live in Buckingham Palace ?
- It was Sunday yesterday. It wasn’t Sunday yesterday. Was Sunday yesterday ?
- They were having breakfast this morning. They weren’t having breakfast this morning. Were they
having breakfast this morning ?
- You are at home. You aren’t at home. Are you at home ?



Ex. 2 : Complète les phrases suivantes avec des question-tags et des short answers. Sois bien attentif
au temps, donc à l’auxiliaire. Pour t’aider, tu peux souligner le verbe qui te donnera l’auxiliaire et le
sujet.

- It was our thief, wasn’t it ? No, it wasn’t.
- You must be proud of you, mustn’t you ? Yes, I must (il s’agit d’un dialogue)
- She was sitting in the front row, wasn’t she ? Yes, she was.
- She is phoning her mother, isn’t she ? No, she isn’t.
- It always rains in this country, doesn’t it ? yes, it does.



Ex. 3 : Pose la bonne question en fonction de l’élément souligné en utilisant un pronom interrogatif.
Sois bien attentif au temps.

- There was a concert yesterday. What was there yesterday ?
- I prefer the blue shoes. Which one do you prefer ?
- She was in London on Monday. When was she in London ?
- She is writing a letter to her friend because she misses her (elle lui manque) Why is she writing a
letter to her friend ?
- They were talking to Mr Smith. Who were they talking to ?
