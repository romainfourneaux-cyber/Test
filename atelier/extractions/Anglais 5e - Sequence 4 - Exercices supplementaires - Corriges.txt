                   Anglais 5e - Exercices supplémentaires - Séquence 4

                                                  Corrigés

Ex. 1 : Mets les phrases suivantes à la forme négative. Sois bien attentif au temps et aux auxiliaires contractés.
She’s got brown eyes. She hasn’t got brown eyes.
She’s playing in the park. She isn’t playing in the park.
They missed the train. They didn’t miss the train.
They go to school by bus. They don’t go to school by bus.
They’ll be at home on time. They won’t be at home on time.

Ex. 2 : Mets les phrases suivantes à la forme interrogative (sans le pronom interrogatif)
She wanted to go to the concert. Did she want to go to the concert ?
They ate a lot of jam (confiture). Did they eat a lot of jam ?
The little girl is listening to a bird singing. Is the little girl listening to a bird singing ?
There’re a lot of people in the street. Are there a lot of people in the street ?
You’ll drink a lot of water when you walk in the mountain. Will you drink a lot of water when you walk in the
mountain ?

Ex. 3 : Transpose les phrases suivantes au futur.
Do you talk to your teacher ? Will you talk to your teacher ?
They read some books. They will read some books. / They’ll read some books.
He didn’t cut the grass in his garden. He won’t cut the grass in his garden.
It is hot by the sea. It will be hot by the sea.

Ex. 4 : Complète ces phrases avec des comparatifs d’égalité ou de supériorité selon l’indication.
My mother isn’t as exhausted as your mother.
William is taller than Mrs Smith
Going to London is quicker than going to Oxford
Driving a car is more difficult than riding a bike.

Ex. 5 : Pose la question dont voici la réponse, en utilisant un pronom interrogatif.
She is running because she is late. Why is she running ?
They were having dinner at half past nine. What time were they having dinner ?
They took the bus because it goes faster than walking. Why did they take the bus ?
They’ll hire a car tomorrow afternoon. When will they hire a car ?

Ex. 6 : Complète les phrases suivantes en utilisant le gérondif uniquement si cela est nécessaire.
She wants to go to the swimming pool.
She likes swimming in the sea.
Why are you travelling by car ?
They hate running.

Ex. 7 : Complète avec much ou many.
There won’t be many people.
How many brothers and sisters have you got ?
I haven’t got much work this week.
Ex. 8 : Complète avec des question-tags et des short answers.
They won’t go to the cinema this afternoon, will they ? No, they won’t
They left their country, didn’t they ? No, they didn’t
Her sister is attending a geography lesson, isn’t she ? Yes, she is
His neighbour usually wakes up very early, doesn’t he ? No, he doesn’t
They are many people on the underground, aren’t there ? No, there aren’t
This bus can be on time, can’t it ? Yes, it can

phrases interrogatives et négatives.

Il n’a pas cassé le miroir. He didn’t break the mirror.
Il a quitté son pays. He left his country.
Nous louerons une voiture rouge après-demain. We shall We will / We’ll hire a red car the day after
tomorrow.
Y aura-t-il beaucoup de monde au concert ? Will there be a lot of people / many people at the concert ?
