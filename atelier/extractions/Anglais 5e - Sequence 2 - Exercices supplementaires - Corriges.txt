                 Anglais - Corrigé des Exercices supplémentaires – Séquence 2


Ex. 1 : Mets ces phrases au prétérit.
- We are going to London by bus. We were going to London by bus.
- Who are you talking to ? Who were you talking to ?
- They are happy, aren’t they ? They were happy, weren’t they ?
- They aren’t playing in the garden. They weren’t playing in the garden.
- He calls his mother. He called his mother.
- Susan doesn’t visit Castle Rock. Susan didn’t visit Castle Rock.
- Do you decide to visit Edinburgh ? Did you decide to visit Edinburgh ?

Ex. 2 : Mets ces phrases - conjuguées au prétérit simple - aux formes interrogative puis négative :

- He brought a new mirror. Did he bring a new mirror ? He didn’t bring a new mirror.

- We bought tickets to go to the theatre. Did we buy tickets to go to the theatre ? We didn’t buy
tickets to go to the theatre.

- They saw a famous singer. Did they see a famous singer ? They didn’t see a famous singer.

- I decided to go to Scotland last year. Did I decide to go to Scotland last year ? I didn’t decide to go
to Scotland last year.
- He painted this painting one year ago. Did he paint this painting one year ago ? He didn’t paint this
painting one year ago.
- They ate in the dining-room. Did they eat in the dining-room ? They didn’t eat in the dining-room.

Ex. 3 : Complète les phrases suivantes avec l’adjectif possessif qui convient.

- She has blue eyes. → her eyes are blue.
- This car has blue doors. → its doors are blue.
- He has dirty trousers (pantalon) . → his trousers are dirty.
- They don’t have good ideas. → their ideas aren’t good.
- Tom has nice parents. → his parents are nice.

Ex. 4 : Remplace les groupes nominaux soulignés par le pronom possessif qui convient.

- His towel is dry, my towel is wet. His towel is dry, mine is wet.

- Our parents are very nice, their parents often shout. Our parents are very nice, theirs often shout.

- I do not have my umbrella, do you have your umbrella ? I do not have my umbrella, do you have
yours ?

- His father is an architect. her father is a doctor. His father is an architect. Hers is a doctor.

- My television is new. My brother’s television is old. My television is new. His is old.

- Our teacher is a woman. Our teacher is a man. Our teacher is a woman. Ours is a man.
Ex. 5 : Complète avec des question-tags puis des short answers :
She likes travelling, doesn’t she ? Yes, she does.
He has got long hair, hasn’t he ? Yes, he has.
They had breakfast at the hotel, hadn’t they ? No, they hadn’t.
We didn’t miss the train, did we ? No, we didn’t.
They are playing the piano together, aren’t they ? Yes, they are.
Your sister can’t play tennis, can she ? No, she can’t.

Ex. 6 : Traduis les phrases suivantes

- Hier, nous étions à la maison. Yesterday, we were at home.
- Nous avons mangé dans la salle à manger. We ate in the dining-room.
- Ils ont vu l’arrêt d’autobus, n’est-ce pas ? They saw the bus stop, didn’t they ?
- Il y avait un concert à Londres lundi dernier. There was a concert in London last Monday.
- Où étiez-vous hier ? Where were you yesterday ?
- Le policier a acheté une nouvelle voiture. The policeman bought a new car.
